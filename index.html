How it works

Three steps, and a real credential between the machine and your data.

01
AI reads the pattern
Your CGM and pump data are scanned for trends worth noticing — highs, lows, timing shifts, variability — the same way a clinician would, just continuously.
02
A CDCES reviews it
Every drafted flag is checked by a Certified Diabetes Care and Education Specialist before it ever reaches you. Nothing goes out unreviewed.
03
You get a clear next step
Not a chart to decode. A plain-language observation and a question worth asking — of yourself, or your provider.
